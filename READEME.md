## Understanding NLP and LLMs
Natural Language Processing: is a broader field focused on enabling compuers to understand, intrepret and generate human language.
                             NLP encompasses many techniques and tasks such a as sentiment analysis,
                             named entity recognition, and machine translation,
Large language Models: are powerful subset of NLP model characterized by their massive size, extensive trainning data,
                       and ability to perform a wide range of language tasks with minimal task-specific training,
                       Models Like the Llama, GPT, claude series are examples of LLMs that have revolutionized what's possible in NLP
